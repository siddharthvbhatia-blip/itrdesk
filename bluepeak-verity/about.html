Why the business exists

Preparation should make review easier, not create another layer of uncertainty.

BluePeak Verity is built for situations where a firm or finance team needs more execution capacity but still wants defined responsibility, controlled access and review-ready workpapers.

01 · TRACEABILITY

Know the source.

Important balances and schedules should remain traceable to the records used to prepare them.

02 · VISIBILITY

Surface exceptions.

Missing evidence, unusual items and reconciling differences should remain visible rather than disappear inside the file.

03 · HANDOFF

Respect the reviewer.

The next professional should be able to understand what was done, what remains open and where to look next.

Delivery model

A controlled extension of the finance function.

The model is designed to work either behind an accounting firm or alongside a growing business, depending on the engagement structure.

Appropriate engagements

Execution with a defined review path.

  • Recurring bookkeeping and month-end support
  • Bank and card reconciliations
  • Catch-up and cleanup
  • Financial reporting support
  • AP, AR and ageing schedules
  • Audit and tax source-data workpapers for professional review

Outside the implied scope

No invented authority.

  • No representation as a US CPA firm
  • No representation as a UK chartered-accountancy practice
  • No attest opinion unless lawfully provided by the appropriate professional
  • No regulated representation unless separately and lawfully authorised
  • No statutory sign-off merely because preparation support was provided
  • No claim of SOC 2, ISO or other certification not actually held

Operating principles

Control is designed into the engagement before the first handoff.

Scope, access, communication rules, file structure, reviewer responsibility and expected outputs should be agreed before recurring delivery begins.

That discipline is what allows offshore support to feel like an extension of a professional finance operation rather than a disconnected task queue.

Start with a controlled pilot

One defined assignment is enough to test the operating model.

Review the workflow, documentation and handoff before committing to recurring capacity.
