Operating principle

Accuracy before appearance.

The work should reconcile before it is presented. Important balances are traced to source records, assumptions are stated and differences are isolated for review.

  • Reconcile totals and supporting records
  • Check dates, signs, classifications and duplicates
  • Separate genuine errors from presentation differences
  • Maintain an open-item and exception trail

Relationship model

Support—not uncontrolled substitution.

The responsible accounting firm, CPA, tax adviser or business owner retains the review and statutory responsibility applicable to the engagement.

  • Scope and sign-off responsibility agreed in advance
  • No regulated representation unless lawfully authorised
  • Client contact only where expressly permitted
  • Work retained in reviewable and transferable form
