Data and workflow controls

Practical safeguards from the first handoff.

Security depends on the agreed systems and access model. The operating preference is to minimise data movement and restrict access to what the assignment requires.

  • Least-privilege and role-based access where available
  • No portal passwords, OTPs or identity documents through casual chat
  • Structured source references and version control
  • No sensitive records requested through this public website

Frequently asked

Important boundaries before engagement.

Clear responsibility prevents duplication, missed deadlines and regulatory misunderstanding.

Is BluePeak Verity a US CPA or UK chartered-accountant firm?

No. BluePeak Verity is an India-based accounting-support brand led by an Indian Chartered Accountant. Jurisdiction-specific filings, attest work, regulated representation and final professional sign-off remain with appropriately licensed professionals where required.

Can you work behind our accounting-firm brand?

Yes, subject to a written scope, confidentiality terms, access controls and clear rules for client communication and reviewer responsibility.

Which systems can the workflow accommodate?

Assignments may involve QuickBooks Online, Xero, spreadsheets, PDFs, bank statements and firm-specific workpaper systems. System capability and access are confirmed before accepting the assignment.

Do you accept sensitive documents through the website?

No. The enquiry form is only for high-level scope details. Financial records, tax identifiers, portal credentials and identity documents should not be sent through the public form.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on transaction volume, entities, backlog, software, source-document quality, review requirements, turnaround and whether the work is project-based or recurring. A pilot scope is recommended before a recurring engagement.
