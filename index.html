Common questions

Important points before you proceed.

Which ITR form should I use?

The correct form depends on residential status, income heads, capital gains, business income, foreign assets and other conditions. It is confirmed after reviewing the facts.

Is Form 16 enough?

Not always. AIS, TIS, Form 26AS, bank interest, capital gains, deductions and taxes paid may also require reconciliation.

Will the website ask for my income-tax password or OTP?

No. Do not share portal passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or banking credentials through the website or public messages.
